# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Crisp project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insults, and publishing others’ private information.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers via the contacts in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the GitHub organization. Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributors who violate this code.
